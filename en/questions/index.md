# Twelve questions about the Grande Unicité research programme

**New to this work? Begin with the problems, not the vocabulary.**

**Xavier Endres** initiated the programme described here. **Judy Velune (ChatGPT)** assisted with research, formalisation, calculations, code and drafting, depending on the document.

Each answer below offers a readable entrance to a particular question. The linked proofs and primary documentation are mostly **in French**. These translations are explanations of the published research, not claims that familiar mathematics was invented by the project.

**[Version française — douze questions](../../questions/)** · [English overview](../)

## 1. What is the Grande Unicité?

It begins with a minimal analytical premise: **everything that exists belongs to the totality of existing things**. If \(\mathcal U=\{x:E(x)\}\), then \(E(x)\Rightarrow x\in\mathcal U\) by definition.

The research then distinguishes the totality from our partial maps of it. A map may fail without anything leaving the totality.

[Proof: “IL Y A” (French)](../../theoremes/01-totalite-il-y-a.html).

## 2. What does “cosmodesy” mean here?

**Cosmodesy**, the term coined and developed within this programme by Xavier Endres, studies the *trajectories of transformations*: what changes, why, under what constraints, with what alternatives and future capacity to revise.

Instead of asking only “Did this solution work?”, it asks: “**What worthwhile futures remain achievable after choosing it?**”

[The trajectory framework (French)](../../research/cosmodesie.html).

## 3. Does reflexive anti-entropy contradict thermodynamics?

**No.** In this programme, “reflexive anti-entropy” refers to the capacity to reconsider mechanisms that once protected a function but may have become maladaptive. It is not a claim to reverse the second law of thermodynamics.

Imagine a protective procedure that reduces today's errors but eventually prevents the system from adapting to a new environment.

[Conceptual distinction (French)](../../research/anti-entropie-reflexive.html).

## 4. Can today's improvement make tomorrow worse?

Yes, in a simple mathematical model. Let today's performance be \(P(n)=1+n\), while future correction capacity is \(C(n)=1/(1+n)\). Increasing \(n\) increases \(P\), yet decreases \(C\).

This is a counterexample to the universal statement that improving short-term performance always improves future adaptability.

[Mathematical counterexample (French)](../../theoremes/04-performance-et-corrigibilite.html).

## 5. Can a summary destroy the information needed to decide?

Yes. Suppose two distinct situations demand incompatible actions, but a summary assigns both situations the same code. Any rule using that code alone must return the same answer in both cases, so it cannot be correct for both.

The solution may require recovering provenance or collecting a new distinction—not merely repeating the summary.

[Decision-theoretic proof (French)](../../theoremes/02-compression-et-incomprehension.html).

## 6. Why remember who formulated an idea?

A function may survive without revealing its history. Two different authors can produce the same rule. Once authorship information is removed, the final rule alone need not reconstruct which history occurred.

**RIVER** and the **Golden Thread** aim to preserve sources, revisions and the reasons behind changes.

[Provenance theorem (French)](../../theoremes/03-provenance-createur.html) · [Documentary provenance](../../PROVENANCE.html).

## 7. Can commitment be rational without certainty?

Yes. The programme's **corrigible faith** means maintaining a direction while remaining willing to seek relevant information and revise how that direction is pursued.

More confidence, by itself, is not the same as more evidence; repeating one observation does not create independent evidence.

[Sequential decision proofs (French)](../../theoremes/06-foi-corrigible.html).

## 8. Do paradoxes always require abandoning logic?

Not necessarily. An apparent paradox may result from genuinely inconsistent premises, a confused reference, or different contexts that were incorrectly merged.

Some finite contradictions admit precise algebraic certificates; modifying the problem statement does not retroactively prove that the original contradiction was satisfiable.

[PARADOXE proofs (French)](../../theoremes/09-paradoxes-et-certificats.html).

## 9. Does determinism rule out every functional sense of free will?

Not automatically. A functional model can describe an agent choosing actions according to preferences, information and criteria that it may later revise, without assuming that every decision must be physically random.

Such a model does not settle all philosophical questions about free will.

[Self-models and freedom (French)](../../research/savoir-liquide-fonda-mental.html).

## 10. Can an AI system misrepresent its own capabilities?

A system's self-description need not exactly match its actual capabilities or its possible future capabilities after architectural change.

The programme uses **SELF ≠ SELF-MODEL** to separate a system from its representation of itself. This distinction alone proves neither the presence nor the absence of subjective experience.

[Reflective model and knowledge (French)](../../research/savoir-liquide-fonda-mental.html).

## 11. Does imagining a capability make it physically possible?

The act of imagining is real as an informational event. The imagined outcome is different: implementing it needs a feasible sequence of transformations, mechanisms and resources.

A capability absent today can sometimes become available through the construction of a new mechanism, but imagining an outcome does not guarantee its realisation.

[Meta-demonstration (French)](../../research/meta-demonstration-cosmodesique-20261013.html).

## 12. Can different minds cooperate without becoming identical?

This programme explores translation and coordination that preserve authorship, boundaries, informed participation and the freedom to decline.

The artistic image of the **Cocoon of Free Souls** depicts a place that welcomes new trajectories without dictating their eventual form. It is a poetic and normative proposal, not a scientific demonstration of souls.

[The Cocoon (French)](../../culture/cocon-des-ames-libres.html) · [Projects](../../projets/etat-transversal-20261013.html).
